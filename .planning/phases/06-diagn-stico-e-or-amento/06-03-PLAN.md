## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-05 | Tampering | `publishQuoteVersion` | high | mitigate | Use a single transaction that snapshots commercial fields and prevents in-place mutation of published version rows. |
| T-06-06 | Information Disclosure | `renderQuotePdf` | high | mitigate | Render only customer-facing snapshot DTO fields and exclude internal notes, product cost, margin, supplier, audit and token fields. |
| T-06-07 | Information Disclosure | `createQuoteApprovalLink` | high | mitigate | Generate opaque token, persist only hash, omit token/hash from audit payloads and return token only in the authenticated response. |
| T-06-08 | Repudiation | manual `Enviado` and cancel status | medium | mitigate | Persist status transitions with sanitized audit rows and no communication delivery/read claims. |
| T-06-09 | Denial of Service | PDF route | low | accept | PDF renders one tenant-owned published quote at a time; large-file upload or arbitrary template input is not accepted in this phase. |
</threat_model>
