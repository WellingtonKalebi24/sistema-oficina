## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-23 | Information Disclosure | download route | critical | mitigate | DB tenant/checkIn/attachment lookup before streaming; deleted rows non-downloadable. |
| T-05-24 | Tampering | storage path | high | mitigate | Generate storedName and verify resolved path stays under upload root. |
| T-05-25 | Denial of Service | multer upload | high | mitigate | Route-scoped limits for size, files and fields. |
| T-05-26 | Repudiation | upload/delete | medium | mitigate | Audit upload and delete with metadata only, no bytes. |
| T-05-27 | Elevation of Privilege | attachment routes | high | mitigate | Separate backend permissions for read/write/delete per D-11. |
</threat_model>
